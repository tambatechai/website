Where the name comes from

Adapt. Transform. Scale.

Tamba comes from the tambaqui.

tambaquitahm·bah·KEE

Most technology companies are named after an abstract idea or an invented word. Ours is named after a fish.

The tambaqui is one of the great fish of the Amazon. It lives in a river that rises and falls by meters every year, and it has learned to thrive in both seasons. What it does to survive there is, almost point by point, what we want to do for the companies we work with.

Adapt

In the river
When the water rises, it follows it into the flooded forest. When it drops, it goes back to the lakes. Few fish handle that change so well.
In our work
Technology has to keep up with new data, new markets and new customers. We fit each solution to the operation, not the other way around.

Transform

In the river
It feeds on the fruits and seeds that fall from the trees and turns them into energy.
In our work
We turn raw data and scattered information into decisions and results you can measure.

Spread

In the river
The seeds it swallows travel with it and grow somewhere else. It is one of the ways the forest regrows.
In our work
What works in one company reaches the next. And inside each company, what works for one team reaches the whole operation.

Connect

In the river
It moves between rivers, lakes and forest, linking parts of the ecosystem that would otherwise stay apart.
In our work
We connect people, data and systems, and we connect companies in the US and in Brazil.

Tamba is not the product. It is the story behind the company.

That is also why we are Tamba Tech and not Tamba AI. AI is where we start today, not the limit of what we will build. The story stays the same as the technology changes.
